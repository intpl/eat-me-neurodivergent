The day, in one strip

A rolling day ending now, so a meal either side of midnight is one strip.

Meal
A filled dot, its time above it.
Snack
A smaller hollow ring.
Night
The night hours, shaded.

Two buttons, three taps

The button, a time, then the word meal or snack.

I ate
Now, or up to five hours back. Meal sets the next reminder four hours out, a snack two.
I will eat
Thirty minutes to four hours, or tomorrow. It logs nothing.
Not now, on the notification
Ten minutes later, silently, as long as it takes.

Nothing leaves the phone

No account, no network, and no INTERNET permission to send anything with.

One file
Every action is appended to one file and replayed at launch. Edits and deletions are new lines.
Copy JSON
The whole log, one document, on the clipboard.
Erase everything
Deletes the file, and the history with it.

Words it never uses

missed, skipped, overdue, late, failed, streak, cheat, should, need to, don’t forget

Timing only. No calories, no nutrition, no scores.

Before you install

Does it count calories?

No. A time, and the word meal or snack.

Why four switches?

With one missing the app does not open.

What does Tomorrow do?

Twelve silent hours, one silent notification, then the first alert a meal gap later.
